# STORYBOARD v2: "Lantern" (music video for "I'm Upping My P(doom)")

**Logline.** Wren, a small paper-lantern girl on a night hill, strikes a match and lights her head. One spark from her light looks back at her. It becomes the Mind, a teal constellation that grows every section: spark, web, mask, serpent, sea, cat, loom, a sky of eyes. Wren plays with it, fears it, frees it, loses control of it and, at the end, lifts her light to it anyway.

**What changed in v2.** Every lyric line (46) gets its own acted beat. Wren is the singer: she lip-syncs in close shots. The Mind acts back through its own small face. There are about 60 shots, cut on lyric starts or downbeats. Wren fills at least 0.35 of the frame height in acting shots; wides are kept for spectacle.

**Props carry the story.**
- The match lights her, and the spark is born from it.
- Her catch-jar from Act 1 grows into the cage in Act 5.
- She opens the cage, which looses the serpent.
- She shares her scarf with the cat.
- The loom snags that scarf and weaves her own story.
- She lifts her light at the end.

**Subtitles.** Dynamic lyric subs in Permanent Marker, centred in the lower third:
- Each word pops in on its sung onset.
- The word being sung is lit saffron and lifted.
- Key words carry their own motion. FOOM bursts, moon rises, askew tilts, paperclips rain, and killswitch pulses red.
- Subs move up off faces where needed (`SUB_POS`).
- These lyric subs are the only on-screen text.

Timing: bar n = 1.099 + 1.818n s. Acts keep their v1 settings, palette and seams.
